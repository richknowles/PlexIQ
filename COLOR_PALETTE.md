## Color Usage Philosophy

**1930s Chicago Aesthetic:**
- Gold = Prohibition-era glamour, speakeasy luxury
- Dark browns = Shadowy, clandestine atmosphere
- Red accents = Danger, aggression, tommy guns
- Muted earth tones = Period-appropriate restraint

**Functional Color Coding:**
- Warmer colors (gold/red) = More aggressive deletion
- Cooler colors (blue-gray) = Conservative, safe settings
- Brightness increases with intensity
